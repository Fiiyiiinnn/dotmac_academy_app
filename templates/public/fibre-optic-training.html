Fibre optic training in Nigeria

Learn about fibre networks through Dotmac Technical Academy’s Fiber Engineering course. The course has 32 chapters, chapter tests and a final assessment.

Who can apply for free training?

Dotmac Technical Academy offers free technical training to eligible SIWES students, NYSC corps members and OND/HND interns. Confirm Fiber Engineering availability for your intake before applying.

How is training delivered?

The technical programme combines self-paced study with hands-on learning. Check the current intake for practical dates, location and equipment requirements.

How to apply

Choose a training track on the application form and follow the emailed instructions for your entrance assessment.
